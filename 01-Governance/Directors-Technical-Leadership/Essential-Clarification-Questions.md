

## Essential Clarification Questions:

### **Legal & Compliance Foundation:**
1. **Data Controller Status**: Who will be the legal data controller for this project across 400,000+ religious institutions? Is this a centralized entity or distributed model?

2. **Legal Basis for Processing**: What is the specific legal basis under GDPR Article 6 for processing sacramental records, funeral data, and donation information across multiple EU member states? How will you obtain explicit consent from each institution and their members?

3. **Religious Data Classification**: Under GDPR Article 9, religious data is "special category data" with strict processing limitations. What specific exemptions or derogations will you rely on for each member state?

4. **Institutional Consent Process**: How will you obtain documented consent from each of the 400,000+ religious institutions before registering their data? What opt-out mechanisms will be available?

### **Technical & Ethical Concerns:**
5. **Blockchain vs. Right to Erasure**: GDPR's "right to be forgotten" fundamentally conflicts with blockchain immutability. How do you plan to resolve this legal contradiction for donation tracking?

6. **Sacramental Record Security**: What legitimate need requires NATO SECRET-equivalent encryption for church websites? This level of security is typically reserved for national defense systems, not religious institution portals.

7. **Data Minimization**: What specific data elements will be collected from each institution? How does this comply with GDPR's data minimization principle?

### **Project Scope & Ownership:**
8. **Institutional Relationships**: Are these 400,000+ institutions voluntarily participating, or is this a mandatory registration system? What is the business model - are institutions paying for this service?

9. **Domain Ownership**: Who owns/controls the domain names mentioned (gyvenimo-kelias.lt, dzives-cels.lv, elu-tee.ee)? Do you have written authorization from each national church authority?

10. **Regulatory Approvals**: Have you consulted with Data Protection Authorities (DPAs) in Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia regarding this project's compliance framework?
