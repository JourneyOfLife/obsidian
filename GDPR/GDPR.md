**Key Data Protection Requirements for Religious Entities**

Under Article 9 of the GDPR, data concerning religious beliefs is classified as a **special category of personal data**, requiring higher protection standards. Counsel will typically assist with: 

- **Article 91 GDPR Compliance:** This specific article allows religious associations that had their own data protection rules before the GDPR to continue applying them, provided they are brought into alignment with the GDPR.
- **Internal Governance:** Crafting internal privacy policies that protect member data while allowing for the "lawful activity" of a non-profit religious organization.
- **DPO Engagement:** Determining if a **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** is required due to the large-scale processing of sensitive "special category" data.
